AI/ML Engineer (Entry Level)

Location: Delhi NCR, India — hybrid
Team: Applied AI
Experience: 0–2 years

About the role

We build and ship AI systems that run in production, not in notebooks. You will
work on computer vision and language models end to end: training them, wrapping
them in an API, putting them on a VM, and keeping them alive when they break at
2am. This is a role for someone early in their career who has already shipped
something real.

What you will do

- Build and deploy vision and NLP models into production services
- Wrap models behind FastAPI services and keep latency predictable
- Own the deployment path: Docker, CI/CD, reverse proxy, TLS, cloud VMs
- Debug model failures on real user data and fix them at the root
- Write evaluation harnesses so we know when a change made things worse

Required

- Strong Python
- PyTorch or TensorFlow, with models you have actually trained
- Computer vision or NLP experience — detection, classification, OCR,
  embeddings, or similar
- FastAPI or Flask for serving models
- Docker
- SQL

Nice to have

- Azure, AWS, or GCP
- Hugging Face Transformers
- LangChain or similar LLM orchestration tooling
- Retrieval-augmented generation
- CI/CD pipelines
- Nginx

Who this is for

Internships, research work, and substantial personal projects all count as
experience. We care about what you have built and whether you can explain the
decisions behind it.
